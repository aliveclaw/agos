## Reliability Gated Multi-Teacher Distillation

**Priority:** medium
**Module:** intent
**Risk:** medium
**Paper:** Reliability Gated Multi-Teacher Distillation for Low Resource Abstractive Summarization

### Description
A knowledge distillation approach that uses inter-teacher agreement to dynamically route supervision between multiple teacher models and gold labels at the token level. The EWAD mechanism weights distillation based on entropy and teacher consensus, while CPDP maintains geometric constraints on student model positioning relative to heterogeneous teachers.

### How to Apply
This could improve agos's Intent Engine by training smaller, more efficient models that can understand natural language intents with higher reliability. The multi-teacher approach could create specialized intent understanding models that are more robust and require less computational resources while maintaining high accuracy.

### Implementation Hint
Train lightweight intent classification models using multiple teacher LLMs (Claude, GPT, etc.) with reliability gating to improve intent understanding accuracy. Implement the EWAD mechanism to weight different teacher outputs based on their agreement levels, allowing the system to use smaller models for common intents while maintaining quality.
