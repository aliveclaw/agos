## Neuromorphic Decision Dynamics

**Priority:** high
**Module:** coordination
**Risk:** medium
**Paper:** Neuromorphic Realization of Best Response in Finite-Action Games

### Description
A dynamical systems approach that models decision-making as an internal state-space process with stable attractors representing decisions, rather than external choice rules. The system naturally handles commitment formation, symmetry breaking, and decision persistence under perturbations with exponential convergence.

### How to Apply
This could dramatically improve agos's multi-agent coordination by replacing brittle voting/consensus mechanisms with neuromorphic dynamics that naturally resolve conflicts and maintain stable team decisions. The geometry-aware utility computation could help agents converge on optimal coordination strategies even with complex relational structures between possible actions.

### Implementation Hint
Implement a neuromorphic decision layer in the coordination module that models agent choices as dynamical states with relational coupling between options. Use the exponential convergence properties to quickly resolve team coordination decisions while maintaining stability under perturbations from new evidence or changing conditions.
