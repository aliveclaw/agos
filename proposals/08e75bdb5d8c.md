## Reasoning Coherence Validation

**Priority:** medium
**Module:** intent
**Risk:** medium
**Paper:** MME-CoF-Pro: Evaluating Reasoning Coherence in Video Generative Models with Text and Visual Hints

### Description
A systematic approach to validate that multi-step reasoning processes maintain causal consistency across sequential steps, using intermediate reasoning step verification and coherence scoring. The technique involves evaluating whether each reasoning step logically follows from previous steps and maintains consistency throughout the process.

### How to Apply
This could significantly improve agos's Intent Engine by validating that execution plans maintain logical coherence across steps, preventing contradictory or causally inconsistent actions. It would also enhance the Knowledge System by ensuring that inferences stored in the knowledge graph maintain reasoning consistency over time.

### Implementation Hint
Implement a reasoning coherence validator that checks each step in execution plans for logical consistency with previous steps, using a scoring mechanism similar to the paper's Reasoning Score. Add coherence validation hooks in the Intent Engine's plan generation and execution phases.
