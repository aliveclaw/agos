## Semantic Knowledge Distillation into Spatial Representations

**Priority:** high
**Module:** knowledge
**Risk:** low
**Paper:** BEVLM: Distilling Semantic Knowledge from LLMs into Bird's-Eye View Representations

### Description
This technique distills semantic knowledge from LLMs into spatially-structured representations (like Bird's-Eye View) that maintain geometric coherence while preserving rich semantic understanding. It creates unified spatial encodings that combine the reasoning power of LLMs with consistent geometric structure.

### How to Apply
This could dramatically improve agos's spatial reasoning capabilities by creating semantically-rich spatial representations of the agent's workspace and environment. Instead of processing information independently across different contexts, agos could maintain a unified spatial understanding that combines semantic knowledge with geometric relationships.

### Implementation Hint
Extend the Knowledge System to include spatial representations that encode both semantic relationships and geometric/topological structure. Use the LLM to distill semantic meaning into these spatial encodings, creating a unified representation that maintains both semantic richness and spatial consistency across different agent contexts and workspaces.
