## Constrained Decoding for Natural Language Generation

**Priority:** medium
**Module:** intent
**Risk:** medium
**Paper:** A Generative AI Framework for Intelligent Utility Billing CO 2 Analytics and Sustainable Resource Optimisation

### Description
Uses constrained decoding policies to generate natural language outputs from structured data while ensuring outputs meet specific requirements, constraints, or formats. This technique guides the generative AI to produce compliant, readable text that adheres to domain-specific rules and standards.

### How to Apply
Could enhance agos's Intent Engine to generate more controlled and predictable natural language responses when converting execution plans back to user-readable status updates or explanations. This would ensure agos communications are always appropriate, compliant with policies, and formatted correctly for different contexts.

### Implementation Hint
Implement constraint templates in the Intent Engine that define allowed vocabulary, sentence structures, and content rules. Add a post-processing layer that validates LLM outputs against these constraints before returning responses to users.
