## Social Observation Pattern Completion

**Priority:** medium
**Module:** coordination
**Risk:** medium
**Paper:** A Generative Model of Conspicuous Consumption and Status Signaling

### Description
A mechanism where agents observe social interactions and use predictive pattern completion to identify emerging status symbols and cultural conventions. The technique creates feedback loops between observation, prediction, and behavior adaptation that can drive the emergence of new social norms and signaling behaviors.

### How to Apply
This could enable agos agents to dynamically learn and adapt to changing social contexts within multi-agent teams, automatically identifying which behaviors or outputs are valued by the group and adjusting their strategies accordingly. It would make agent coordination more socially intelligent and culturally adaptive.

### Implementation Hint
Extend the multi-agent coordination module to track behavioral patterns and outcomes across agent interactions, then use the LLM to predict which behaviors are gaining social significance. Store these insights in the knowledge system and influence future agent decision-making through the intent engine.
