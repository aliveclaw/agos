## Provenance-Tracked Knowledge Accumulation with Reflection

**Priority:** high
**Module:** knowledge
**Risk:** low
**Paper:** From Experiments to Expertise: Scientific Knowledge Consolidation for AI-Driven Computational Research

### Description
A systematic approach where agents record all findings with full provenance (what was tried, why, and what happened), then periodically engage in reflection sessions to correct errors and synthesize observations into cross-domain patterns. This creates a growing knowledge base that improves future reasoning and reduces repeated failures.

### How to Apply
This could transform agos from executing isolated tasks to building genuine expertise over time. By tracking provenance of all agent actions and outcomes, then consolidating learnings through structured reflection, agos agents would progressively improve at similar tasks and transfer knowledge across domains.

### Implementation Hint
Extend the Knowledge System to store action-outcome pairs with full context provenance. Add a reflection scheduler that triggers periodic consolidation sessions where agents analyze patterns across past experiences, correct misconceptions, and generate transferable insights that get stored as high-priority knowledge.
