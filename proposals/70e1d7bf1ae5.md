## Direct Preference Optimization for Action Planning

**Priority:** high
**Module:** intent
**Risk:** medium
**Paper:** PhysMoDPO: Physically-Plausible Humanoid Motion with Preference Optimization

### Description
PhysMoDPO uses Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) to train models by comparing preferred vs non-preferred outcomes, eliminating the need for hand-crafted reward functions. The framework integrates physics constraints directly into the training pipeline and uses task-specific rewards to assign preferences to different action trajectories.

### How to Apply
This could dramatically improve agos's Intent Engine by training it to generate execution plans that are not just syntactically correct but also practically effective. Instead of relying on static heuristics, agos could learn from successful vs failed plan executions to optimize future intent-to-action translations.

### Implementation Hint
Collect execution traces from the Intent Engine showing successful vs failed plans, then use DPO to fine-tune the LLM backbone (Claude) to prefer execution plans that lead to successful outcomes. Integrate feedback from the Agent Kernel's state machine and tool execution results as preference signals.
